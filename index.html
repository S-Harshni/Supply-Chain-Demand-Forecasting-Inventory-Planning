Supply Chain Demand & Inventory Planner

How much will we sell, which products are about to run out, and what happens if demand or supplier lead times change?

Monthly demand

Units ordered per month, excluding cancelled and suspected-fraud orders.

Seasonality index

Average weekly demand in each month of 2015-2016 divided by the overall weekly average (1.00 = normal).

Forecast vs actual (backtest)

Total weekly units for the 9 core products; each forecast only uses data from before its date.

Forecast accuracy

WAPE = total absolute error / total demand. Lower is better. The 4-week total is what replenishment depends on.

Inventory value over time

Stock value at cost during the simulation on real weekly demand.

Products by status

As of the planning date, using the next-4-week forecast.

Reorder recommendations

Reorder point = forecast demand over lead time + review week, plus safety stock at a 95% service level.

Late shipments by shipping mode

Share of shipments that took longer than promised.

Promised vs actual days

Average days per shipping mode.

Late rate over time

Monthly share of late shipments.

Late rate by market

All shipping modes combined.

What-if scenarios

The reorder policy stays as planned while real demand or the supplier's real lead time changes. Simulated on 28 weeks of real demand for the 9 core products.

Fill rate by scenario

Data cleaning log

Every cleaning step is counted, so nothing changes silently.

Data: DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset (Constante, Silva & Pereira, Mendeley Data, CC BY 4.0). Stock levels are simulated from real demand with stated lead-time and cost assumptions.
Built with Python, pandas, SQL (SQLite), scikit-learn and Chart.js. A Power BI version of this dashboard is described in the repository.
